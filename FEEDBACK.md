## What kind of feedback is useful?

### Bug reports

If something doesn't work as expected, please include:

- **Phone model and Android version** (e.g., Pixel 8 Pro, Android 15)
- **App version** (visible in the main screen or in the APK filename)
- **What you were doing** when the problem occurred
- **What happened** vs. what you expected to happen
- **Steps to reproduce** the issue, if possible

Example:

> Phone: Samsung Galaxy S23, Android 14  
> App version: 2.1.2  
> Problem: GPS doesn't turn off after disconnecting Bluetooth  
> Steps: Connected to car Bluetooth → GPS turned on → Disconnected → GPS stayed on for 10+ minutes

### Feature requests

If you have an idea for a new feature or improvement:

- **Describe the feature** — what should it do?
- **Explain the use case** — why would it be useful?
- **Suggest how it could work** — any ideas on implementation?

Example:

> Feature: Schedule-based GPS control  
> Use case: I want GPS to turn on automatically every weekday at 8 AM (commute time) even without Bluetooth  
> How: Add a "Scheduled triggers" section with time-based rules

### General feedback

Anything else is welcome:

- Suggestions to improve the documentation
- UI/UX improvements
- Performance observations
- Success stories (always nice to hear!)
